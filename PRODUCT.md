# Lisbon Atlas

## Platform

Web, published through GitHub Pages.

## Purpose and users

This portfolio supports a Safari and Edge memory/performance demonstration.
Real content, photographs, genuinely completed loading and retained browser tabs
matter more than typography fidelity. The individual pages must look like real
websites, without demo indicators.

## Confirmed scope

The 30 existing Lisbon properties retain their seven original sections.
Their isolated speed copies visit Home, Detail, About and Offerings.
The additional Dom Luís I Bridge page is a single photo-heavy page, not another
seven-section property. It is available in the original site and appended as
the final, 121st speed page.

## Bridge page

The audience is enthusiasts of bridges around the world. The subject is
Dom Luís I Bridge in Porto, between Porto and Vila Nova de Gaia.
Use the user's PortoBridge photographs selectively to show different angles;
use the supplied Illustration.jpg as a home-page visualization.
Assets are user-supplied; no new licensing or ownership claims are implied.

## Constraints

Use system fonts in speed mode. Preserve the original sites, supplied video,
real resource/error checks, automatic-start URL and tab retention.
Windows WebKit verification must not be presented as actual Mac Safari testing.
